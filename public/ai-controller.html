📋 Quick Start

  1. Open the game at localhost:3000 in another tab
  2. Open browser console (F12) and run: localStorage.clear() then refresh
  3. In the console, load the optimal city:
    fetch('/optimal-city-save.json').then(r=>r.json()).then(d=>{localStorage.setItem('isocity-game-state',JSON.stringify(d));location.reload()})
  4. Enable City AI: Click the 🤖 button in the game toolbar
  5. Set speed to 3x for faster simulation
  6. Watch the NPCs trade, the AI build, and the city thrive!
