# Crypto City: Brand Narrative & World Bible

## The Emotional Center

**Core Feeling:** The exhilarating terror of watching your portfolio 10x and then -90% while you're on a roller coaster that's also on fire, and somehow you're having the time of your life.

**The Promise:** Build the city you wish Web3 would become—one where the absurdity is the feature, not the bug.

## The Origin Myth: A Hitchhiker's Guide to the Blockchain

### Prologue: The Genesis Block

> *In the beginning, there was nothing. Which was fine. Nothing had a lot going for it—it was predictable, it didn't require a 24-word seed phrase, and it had never asked anyone to connect their wallet.*
>
> *Then, on an otherwise unremarkable day in 2008, a pseudonymous entity known only as Satoshi Nakamoto—which translates roughly from the Japanese as "He Who Cannot Be Doxxed"—released a white paper. The white paper described a way to send magic internet money to strangers without asking permission from banks, which banks found quite rude.*
>
> *The paper was nine pages long. It would spawn an industry of 900-page pitch decks, none of which would be as useful.*
>
> *And thus began the era of cryptocurrency, or as historians would later call it: "That time humanity invented a new kind of anxiety."*

### The Founding of Crypto City

Crypto City wasn't built in a day. It was built in a weekend during a hackathon, then abandoned for three years, then "relaunched" with a new token, then rugged, then forked, then airdropped to everyone who had ever accidentally connected their wallet to a suspicious website.

The city exists in a quantum state between "We're All Gonna Make It" and "Not Gonna Make It"—technically both, until someone checks CoinGecko.

**Founding Principles (carved into the Genesis Block, then lost in a boating accident):**

1. **Code is Law** — except when governance votes otherwise
2. **Not Your Keys, Not Your Coins** — unless it's on a CEX, then it's "Trust Me Bro"
3. **DYOR** — Do Your Own Research (which means watching a 10-minute YouTube video at 2x speed)
4. **The Number Go Up Technology** — The core innovation

## The Hero's Journey: The Degen's Path

Every citizen of Crypto City follows the same archetypal journey:

### Act I: The Ordinary World (Fiat Prison)
The hero lives in a world of 0.01% savings accounts and banks that close at 3pm on Fridays. They work a job they don't love, pay taxes they don't understand, and dream of a life where numbers on a screen could make them free.

### The Call to Adventure
A friend mentions they 50x'd on a dog coin. The hero laughs. The hero can't sleep. The hero downloads Coinbase at 3am.

### Refusal of the Call
"This is obviously a scam," the hero says, putting $50 in "just to see."

### Crossing the First Threshold
The $50 becomes $200. The hero puts in $500 more. They have now crossed into the Special World.

### Act II: The Road of Trials
- **The First Rug** — A formative experience. The hero learns that "audited" doesn't mean "safe."
- **The Gas Fee Revelation** — The hero pays $200 to swap $50 worth of tokens.
- **The Seed Phrase Incident** — The hero writes their seed phrase on a napkin, loses it, then finds it stuck to the bottom of a coffee cup. 
- **The Twitter Space at 3am** — Sleep deprivation becomes a lifestyle.

### The Innermost Cave: The Bear Market
Prices fall 80%. The hero's portfolio becomes a rounding error. But in this dark moment, they discover the true treasure: **they understand the tech now.** Or at least they can explain gas to their family. Badly.

### The Resurrection: The Next Cycle
The hero emerges from the bear market with diamond hands and a 47-page document on why their favorite L2 is the future.

### Return with the Elixir
The hero brings knowledge back to the ordinary world. They can now explain to their parents why they "work in crypto." They can do this for 45 minutes without anyone understanding a word.

## The Five Districts of Crypto City

### 1. The Staking Suburbs (Residential)
Where citizens live in various states of financial exposure.

**The Vibe:** A pleasant neighborhood where everyone knows their APY but not their neighbor's name. Houses range from modest "first airdrop" bungalows to ostentatious whale mansions with more bathrooms than seed phrase backups.

**Sound:** The gentle *ding* of staking rewards hitting wallets at 2am.

### 2. The Trading District (Commercial)
Where commerce happens at the speed of frontrunning.

**The Vibe:** Neon lights, perpetual price tickers, and shops that accept 47 different tokens but somehow never have change. Every transaction includes a small prayer to the liquidity gods.

**Sound:** The collective "OH NO" when someone market sells into thin liquidity.

### 3. The Mining Flats (Industrial)
Where value is extracted and occasionally hashed.

**The Vibe:** Warehouses humming with equipment, farms that "yield" things other than vegetables, and factories where the only emission is carbon. The air smells like burnt graphics cards and existential dread.

**Sound:** GPU fans. So many GPU fans.

### 4. The Civic Quarter (Government/Services)
Where decentralized governance tries to make decisions.

**The Vibe:** Imposing buildings that house DAOs, governance forums, and the eternal debate about whether to burn tokens. Every proposal takes 14 days to vote on and 6 months to implement.

**Sound:** *"But have we considered a temperature check first?"*

### 5. The Degen District (Entertainment/Special)
Where responsible financial decisions go to die.

**The Vibe:** It's always 3am here. Everything is possible. Nothing is audited. The air tastes like hopium and regret.

**Sound:** *"I'm never doing this again,"* followed immediately by, *"Okay, one more ape."*

## The Tone Guide

### Voice Characteristics
- **Sardonic but affectionate** — We're laughing *with* crypto culture
- **Specific references** — The humor lands harder when it's precise
- **Accessible absurdity** — Normies should get the joke, degens should feel seen
- **Optimistic nihilism** — Everything might go wrong, and that's okay
- **Douglas Adams energy** — Deadpan delivery of ridiculous facts

### Example Tone
✅ "The building was audited by a firm that has since been audited. Results pending."
✅ "Residents report 95% satisfaction. The other 5% were liquidated."
✅ "Open 24/7 because markets never sleep and neither can you."

❌ "LMAO get rekt noob" (too mean)
❌ "This is a scam" (too negative)
❌ "Number go up!" (too shallow)

### Easter Eggs & References
Every building description should reward crypto natives with:
- Protocol references ("This building processes transactions with the efficiency of Solana and the decentralization of a well-organized spreadsheet.")
- Historical events ("Built in 2022. Renovated in 2022. Abandoned in 2022. Reopened under new management in 2023.")
- Meme callbacks ("Do Kwon Memorial Wing" in the museum)
- Technical humor ("The elevator uses optimistic verification. We assume you pressed the right floor.")

## The Macro Narrative: Cycles

Crypto City experiences seasons that mirror market cycles:

### The Summer of Leverage
- NPCs are ecstatic
- Buildings construct faster
- New buildings appear spontaneously (like random shitcoin projects)
- Everyone talks about their gains

### The Autumn of Concern
- News tickers show FUD
- Some buildings show warning signs
- NPCs walk a bit slower
- Treasury starts to matter more

### The Winter of Capitulation
- Some buildings go dark (rugged)
- NPCs look tired
- Only the strongest buildings remain
- It's a good time to build quietly

### The Spring of Accumulation
- Bargains appear
- Smart NPCs are back
- Building costs are lower
- Hope returns cautiously

## Appendix: The Glossary (In-Game Codex)

A "Hitchhiker's Guide" style in-game encyclopedia:

> **APY (Absurdly Projected Yield):** A number that tells you how rich you'll be, assuming nothing bad ever happens. Notable examples include Anchor Protocol's 20% "sustainable" yield and Celsius's 18% "definitely real" returns. Nothing bad ever happens. (See: Terra/Luna, Celsius)
>
> **Boating Accident:** The statistically improbable yet remarkably common occurrence where crypto holders lose their hardware wallets to maritime disasters, rendering their holdings conveniently inaccessible to tax authorities. Very sad. Very convenient.
>
> **Degen:** Short for "degenerate," but used affectionately, like how Australians use expletives. The official term for citizens who ape into tokens at 3am based on Twitter recommendations from anonymous accounts.
>
> **Diamond Hands 💎🙌:** The practice of holding an asset until it either makes you rich or worthless. There is no in-between. Originated in WallStreetBets, adopted by crypto. Antonym: Paper Hands.
>
> **DYOR:** "Do Your Own Research." Usually means watching a 10-minute YouTube video at 2x speed. The magic words that protect no one legally but make everyone feel responsible.
>
> **Effective Altruism:** A philosophy of doing the most good, which in 2022 collided spectacularly with doing the most fraud. Use with caution. See: FTX.
>
> **FOMO:** Fear Of Missing Out. The emotion that precedes most bad decisions. The founding principle of the 2021 bull run.
>
> **Gas:** The fee you pay to do anything on the blockchain. Named "gas" because high fees make you want to drive into oncoming traffic. On Ethereum in 2021, sometimes cost more than the transaction itself.
>
> **GM/GN:** Good Morning/Good Night. A community ritual proving you're alive and checking charts. Originated on Crypto Twitter, became NFT culture staple. Response: "GM fren, WAGMI."
>
> **Goxed:** To lose your funds due to exchange failure. Named after Mt. Gox, which lost 850,000 BTC in 2014. "Don't get goxed" was good advice then. Still is.
>
> **Hey Hey Hey:** The official greeting of cautionary tales. Origin: Carlos Matos's legendary Bitconnect presentation (2017). Usage: Ironic enthusiasm, often preceding disaster.
>
> **HODL:** Originally a typo from December 18, 2013, when user GameKyuubi posted "I AM HODLING" on BitcoinTalk while drunk during a price crash. Now a philosophy, lifestyle, and the only strategy most people have.
>
> **Lambo:** The official vehicle of cryptocurrency success. Nobody knows how to drive them, but everyone wants one. "Wen Lambo?" — the eternal question.
>
> **Liquidation:** When the market decides you were wrong in an official, permanent way. The reason the Liquidation Rescue service exists.
>
> **Lunatic:** Originally a term for Terra/Luna supporters. Now used to describe anyone still in those support groups. Not to be confused with "mooning."
>
> **Moon:** Where we're all going. Always. No matter what. Not financial advice. The only acceptable destination for number line.
>
> **NGMI:** "Not Gonna Make It." Usually said about others who sold too early. Never about ourselves. The opposite of WAGMI.
>
> **Probably Nothing:** Ironic understatement indicating something is almost certainly significant. Usage: "Ethereum flipped Bitcoin in daily volume. Probably nothing."
>
> **Rug/Rugged:** When a project's founders take all the money and disappear. Named after the household item you didn't know you needed until it was pulled out from under you. "We got rugged. Again."
>
> **Ser:** Intentional misspelling of "Sir." Used respectfully, often before delivering bad news. "Ser, the peg." — Spoken May 2022 regarding UST.
>
> **Supercycle:** The theory that Bitcoin only goes up forever. Popularized by Three Arrows Capital. See: "Things that aged poorly."
>
> **Unbank Yourself:** Celsius Network's slogan (2017-2022). The process of removing all banking protections from your savings. Results may vary. Results did vary.
>
> **WAGMI:** "We're All Gonna Make It." A statement of faith, community, and collective delusion. Ironically, if everyone actually believed this, it might be true. Originated from bodybuilding forums, adopted by crypto.
>
> **Wen:** Intentional misspelling of "When." Used in "Wen Lambo?" and "Wen Moon?" — Questions without answers.

## Summary: The Three Pillars

1. **EVERYTHING IS A FEATURE** — The volatility, the absurdity, the 3am anxiety spirals. They're not bugs; they're what makes this interesting.

2. **COMMUNITY THROUGH CHAOS** — We're all in this together. The humor comes from shared experience, not mockery.

3. **BUILD ANYWAY** — Despite everything, people build. That's the real story of crypto. That's what Crypto City celebrates.
